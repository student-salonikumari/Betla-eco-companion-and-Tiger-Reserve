Important Visitor Notice

Please follow these safety and forest rules.

🐯
Maintain a safe distance from wildlife and never try to feed or disturb animals.
🚫
Do not litter, use plastic carelessly, or leave waste inside the forest area.
📵
Follow safari instructions and avoid loud sounds that may disturb wildlife.
🆘
In an emergency, use the Emergency Support System services and contact the appropriate emergency authority.
