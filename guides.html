Visitor Information

A few simple tips can make your Betla experience safer, easier and more memorable.

Safari Planning

Choose your safari slot in advance. Morning and evening safaris offer different wildlife experiences.

Respect Nature

Do not litter, feed animals or disturb wildlife. Follow forest department instructions at all times.

Be Wildlife Ready

Carry water, comfortable clothing, binoculars and a camera. Keep your surroundings quiet.

Meet a local expert and discover Betla beyond the usual tourist route.
