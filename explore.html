VERIFIED VISITOR INFORMATION

Before You Visit

EcoCompanion is designed to provide visitors with practical, source-backed information instead of unverified claims.

🌲 Betla: According to the Forest Department, Betla is Jharkhand's only National Park. It covers approximately 226.33 sq km and forms part of the core area of Palamau Tiger Reserve.
🏛 Palamu Fort: According to the Latehar District Administration, the fort complex is around 3 km from Betla National Park and consists of two ruined forts.
💧 Suga Bandh: Government sources place Suga Bandh near Baresand and approximately 60 km from Betla. It lies along the Betla–Netarhat route.
🌄 Netarhat: Jharkhand Tourism describes Netarhat as a hill destination at approximately 3,800 ft elevation, known for sunrise, sunset, pine forests and pear gardens.
⚠️ Waterfall Safety: Rocks can become slippery and water conditions can change quickly during heavy rainfall. Visitors should follow local safety instructions, avoid dangerous edges and never litter.
